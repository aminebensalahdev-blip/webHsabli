Privacy Policy

Last updated:

At Hsabliya (حسبها بسهولة), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Project and calculation data: invoices, glass and aluminum types, prices, and measurements you enter. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Technical data: we may collect basic device and operating system information to improve app performance and fix crashes.
  • Contact data: if you email us, we keep your address and message content solely to reply to you.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the app and calculate materials, costs, and prices.
  • To save and restore your projects when needed.
  • To improve the user experience and fix technical issues.
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Limited data may be shared only with trusted technical service providers (such as crash analytics) to the extent necessary to operate the app.

4. Data Storage and Protection

Most of your data is stored locally on your device. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Your Rights

  • You can delete your projects and data from within the app at any time.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop any processing of your data.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for professionals and craftsmen and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last updated" date above.
